## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, precision-driven e-commerce environment for professional and enthusiast badminton players. Blending the technical rigor of competitive tournament gear with the clean agility of modern sportswear retail, the aesthetic is defined as **High-Contrast Modern Athletic**. 

The UI communicates speed, snap, and tournament-grade authority. Whitespace is deliberate and crystalline, framed by a grounded dark navy anchor structure and energized by targeted hits of high-voltage athletic red. The interface rejects unnecessary embellishments, focusing instead on rapid product discovery, razor-sharp technical specifications (balance points, flex indices, string tension gauges), and friction-free purchasing flows.

## Colors

The color system relies on high-contrast role specialization to balance functional retail hierarchy with athletic energy:

- **Primary Anchor (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate Navy):** Deployed across global top-level navigation, mega-menus, dark footer structures, primary product titles, and high-emphasis contrast cards. It provides structural weight and technical gravitas.
- **Secondary / Kinetic Action (`#EF4444` - Athletic Crimson):** Reserved exclusively for conversion-critical interaction: "Add to Bag", active checkout triggers, discount callout tags, clearance banners, and struck-through sale prices. Never diluted on passive elements.
- **Tertiary / Sport Accent (`#2563EB` - Royal Sport Blue):** Denotes secondary interaction paths, interactive links, informational badges ("Pro Choice", "Custom Stringing Available"), hover borders, focus rings, and active tab indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` - Light Slate Canvas):** Forms the foundational body background, offset by pure `#FFFFFF` for primary product cards and modular containers, and `#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1` for ultra-clean, low-friction dividing rules and input borders.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, labels, and numeric metrics with **Inter** for technical descriptions and tabular specification sheets.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a contemporary, geometric athletic presence with tight counters and energetic diagonal cuts. Headlines utilize bold to extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tightened tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic high-speed typography seen in international tournament broadcasts.
- **Inter** handles utility copy, filtering matrices, string gauge charts, and reviews with neutral clarity and tall x-height, guaranteeing legibility down to micro specifications (`11px` - `13px`).
- Price numbers and metric ratings (e.g., `4U / G5`, `30 LBS`) leverage tabular numbers with `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold weighting for immediate visual scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an 8px rhythmic grid wrapped inside a responsive 12-column structure:

- **Desktop (`1280px` - `1440px+`):** Fixed maximum container width of `80rem` (1280px) with 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` page margins. Product listing pages (PLPs) default to 4 columns for rackets and footwear, or 3 columns when filter sidebars are expanded.
- **Tablet (`768px` - `1024px`):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and collapsible off-canvas filter draws. Product grids collapse gracefully to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (`375px` - `480px`):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins. Product listings feature a tight 2-column grid with compact specs and full-width sticky quick-actions at the base of the viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a crisp, low-diffusion elevation architecture that avoids muddy shadows and emphasizes athletic precision. Depth is created through crisp surface boundaries layered against `#F8FAFC`.

- **Rest Elevation:** Pure white product cards sit on the light slate canvas with an ultra-subtle ambient boundary: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Active Elevation (Product Card Hover):** On pointer hover, product cards translate vertically (`translateY(-4px)`) and cast an intensified athletic drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Card borders transition seamlessly from `#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Navigation & Overlays:** The primary navy header (`#0F172A`) utilizes a flat profile at rest and casts `0 4px 20px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)` when fixed/sticky on scroll. Mega-menus and filter drawers use high-elevation directional drop shadows (`0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`) over crisp `1px` structural borders.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft / Technical (`1`)** shape language. Elements feature intentional, tightly controlled radii that preserve the feel of speed, high-tension rackets, and engineered synthetic equipment:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Badges:** Default radius of `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px).
- **Product Cards, Modals, and Flyout Panels:** Medium radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Metric Indicator Pills and Sale Tags:** Full pill format (`9999px`) is strictly reserved for small sale percentage badges (`-30%`) and status tags ("In Stock", "Pre-strung") to quickly contrast with the angular card frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Bag", "Proceed to Checkout"):** Solid Athletic Crimson (`#EF4444`) with pure white text, bold `label-lg`, `0.375rem` border-radius, and micro-padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state shifts to deeper crimson (`#DC2626`) with a slight upward lift. Focus rings trigger a sharp `2px` offset in `#2563EB`.
- **Secondary CTA ("Customize Stringing", "Quick View"):** Solid `#0F172A` Navy background with white text, or transparent with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#0F172A` and text in `#0F172A`. Hover shifts background to `#1E293B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Actions:** Borderless `#2563EB` text with an underline transition on hover, applied to specification downloads and warranty links.

### Badges & Tags
- **Sale & Discount:** Vibrant `#EF4444` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` tabular numbers, uppercase bold `label-sm`, pill-shaped (`9999px`).
- **Technical & Feature Badges ("Head-Heavy", "Stiff Flex", "Tournament Approved"):** Subtle light slate or soft blue tint background (`#EFF6FF`) with `#2563EB` text and a subtle `1px` border (`#BFDBFE`), `0.25rem` radius.

### Product Cards
- Built on a crisp `#FFFFFF` foundation with a `1px` border in `#E2E8F0` and `0.5rem` radius.
- Includes a dedicated upper badge row (Discounts left, Favorite toggle right), an uncluttered center stage for racket/shoe photography on neutral transparent backings, followed by racket weight/grip classifications (`3U/G5`), bold product titles in `#0F172A`, and bottom-aligned price displays.
- Clearance and sale cards highlight the reduced price in bold `#EF4444`, pairing it with a struck-through original price in `#64748B`.
- Hover activates the sharp vertical shift and drop-shadow, revealing a fast "Quick Add" trigger.

### Search Bar & Header
- Grounded on the dark navy `#0F172A` header container.
- The search field features an inset crisp pill/soft-rect input container (`#1E293B`) with light text (`#F8FAFC`), placeholder `#94A3B8`, and an integrated search icon. Focus expands the border with `#2563EB` glow and launches an instantaneous predictive dropdown highlighting popular racket models, grip sizes, and shuttlecock speed ratings.

### Mega-Menu Navigation
- Multi-tier dropdown anchored to the main `#0F172A` bar. 
- Features category divisions ("Rackets by Playstyle: Head-Heavy / Even-Balance / Head-Light", "Shoes by Court Surface", "Strings & Tension Services") complemented by a featured promo card displaying tournament-edition releases.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Form fields and specification dropdowns (tension selector `20-35 lbs`, grip size `G4, G5, G6`) use `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px` `#CBD5E1` border, `0.375rem` border radius, and dark slate typography. Active focus immediately transitions the border to `#2563EB` with an athletic `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` ring.
